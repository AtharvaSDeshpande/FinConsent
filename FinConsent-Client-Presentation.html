Industry Landscape
The Problem: High-Stakes Friction in Indian Wealth Advisory
Why legacy approval channels fail both advisory practices and high-net-worth investors.

1. Informal WhatsApp Approvals

  • Advisers routinely collect approvals via WhatsApp texts, email replies, or unrecorded phone calls.
  • SEBI inspection teams reject informal text messages as non-compliant with Regulation 19 record-keeping rules.
  • Leaves advisers legally exposed to claims of discretionary or unauthorized trading.
  • Inadmissible under Section 63 BSA / Section 65B without certified cryptographic hash continuity and device provenance.

2. Capital Gains Tax Shocks

  • Finance Act 2024 revised STCG to 20% and LTCG to 12.5% (+ 4% cess).
  • Rebalancing portfolios triggers significant capital gains without prior investor visibility.
  • Clients are blindsided by unexpected year-end tax liabilities.
  • Advance tax penal interest (Sections 208, 234B & 234C where liability >= ₹10,000) erodes investor trust and fee satisfaction.

3. Fragmented Audit Trails

  • Advisory rationale, client communications, and trade executions live in separate silos.
  • Compiling regulatory records for annual SEBI audits takes weeks of administrative overhead.
  • Audit logs in spreadsheets can be altered, disputed, or deleted.
  • Inability to prove exact IST timestamp, client IP, and portfolio allocation agreed upon.

Data Privacy Gate
Mandate Security: Password-Protected Access Gate
Zero sensitive financial disclosure prior to client authentication.
Figure 10.1: Client Password Gate on Mobile. Sensitive portfolio holdings remain encrypted until unlocked.

PAN + DOB Password Mechanism

  • Zero PII Exposure Prior to Unlock: If a magic link is forwarded, intercepted, or leaked, the recipient sees only the adviser's firm name. Zero stock holdings or tax figures are emitted.
  • Deterministic Verification PIN: Password formatted as Last 4 PAN digits + Date of Birth (DDMMYYYY). Example: Rohan's PAN XXXXX1234F + 14/08/1978 = 123414081978.
  • PBKDF2 Key Derivation: The password derives a 256-bit cryptographic key using 100,000 SHA-256 iterations to unwrap the Data Encryption Key (DEK).
  • Online & Offline Defenses: Acknowledges finite PIN entropy (~10^12 combinations); defended online by sliding-window rate limiters (5 failed attempts -> 15 min lockout) and offline by salted PBKDF2.

Cryptographic Core
Data Security Architecture & Dual-Envelope Encryption
Institutional cryptographic defense protecting financial mandates at rest and in transit.

Payload Encryption

  • Algorithm: AES-256-GCM
  • Key: Unique 256-bit random Data Encryption Key (DEK) per mandate.
  • IV: 12-byte random initialization vector.
  • Auth Tag: 16-byte authentication tag ensuring ciphertext integrity and anti-tampering.

Dual Key Wrapping

  • Server Wrapping: DEK encrypted with isolated Server Master Key (SERVER_MASTER_KEY) via AES-256-GCM for RIA dashboard access.
  • Client Wrapping: DEK encrypted with key derived from PBKDF2(PAN_last_4 + DOB, salt, 100,000 rounds).
  • Zero Plaintext: Neither plaintext DEK nor client password stored in DB.

Transit & Storage

  • Magic Token Hashing: 256-bit raw token stored in DB strictly as SHA-256 digest (magicTokenHash).
  • In-Transit Security: Forced TLS 1.3 with Helmet HSTS (1-year max-age: 31,536,000 seconds).
  • Zero Secret Logs: Passwords, OTPs, and bearer tokens sanitized from all logs.

Statutory Authorization Safeguards

  • Mandatory Declarations: Clients must actively check 3 statutory declarations (Regulation 19 Informed Consent, Capital Gains Acceptance, Section 63 BSA Electronic Evidence).
  • Dynamic 6-Digit OTP: Generated via CSPRNG with 5-minute TTL; delivered via registered email and in-app display (SMS disabled by policy; WhatsApp roadmap).
  • Brute-Force Lockout: Maximum 3 verification attempts permitted. A 3rd failed attempt immediately locks verification and burns the token (HTTP 429).
  • Single-Use Atomic Revocation: The instant consent succeeds, the magic token is burned. Re-accessing the URL returns HTTP 410 Gone, preventing replay attacks.

Advance Tax Planning & Caveats (Sec 208 & 234C)

Advance tax applies when estimated net annual tax liability reaches or exceeds ₹10,000, displaying statutory installment deadlines:

  • 1st Installment (15%): On or before 15th June
  • 2nd Installment (45%): On or before 15th September
  • 3rd Installment (75%): On or before 15th December
  • 4th Installment (100%): On or before 15th March
  • Proviso to Section 234C: Relieves penal interest for installments prior to the capital gains realization date if remaining tax is paid in subsequent installments.

Threat Modeling
Security Threat Scenarios & Evidenced Protections
Proven runtime boundary enforcement against malicious and accidental vectors.
Figure 20.1: Security Test Evidence — OTP lockout engaged after 3 failed verification attempts.

Threat Modeling & Defenses

  • Credential Stuffing & OTP Brute-Force: Sliding-window rate limiters block rapid bursts; 3 failed OTPs permanently lock the token (HTTP 429).
  • Cross-Tenant IDOR: Tampering with mandate or client UUIDs across RIA practices rejected with HTTP 403 Forbidden.
  • Expired / Replay Attacks: Opening links past the 48-hour cutoff or after successful consent returns HTTP 410 Gone / Revoked.
  • Token Sniffing & Database Breaches: Plaintext magic tokens are never persisted in the database (stored strictly as SHA-256 hashes).

Section 63 BSA Electronic Evidence Mandatory Attributes

Under Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA) and Section 65B of erstwhile Indian Evidence Act, secondary electronic records require:

  • 1. Origin Identification: Client IP Address (RFC 5737 203.0.113.12) and browser User-Agent recorded at execution time.
  • 2. Synchronized Timestamp: Server-side NTP timestamping localized to Indian Standard Time (IST).
  • 3. Hash Integrity Seal: SHA-256 payloadHash computed over the immutable consent certificate.
  • 4. Device Custody Proof: Evidence that the computer system was operating properly during record generation.
  • 5. Regulation 19 Admissibility: Cryptographically chained forward hashes guarantee zero retroactive modifications.
